the Great
Black River Trip

Are you looking for something that looks cool, but not scary? This is the perfect path for you! A nice gentle flow, albeit rocky the rocks are quite smooth and flat, this poses little danger even to beginners, although still presents a challenge. With dark smooth rocks, it looks black, hence the name. It also highlights the ripples in the water.

the Great
Angel River Trip

If your looking for something actioned path, look to the right, but if you want to spend time to be still and see the scerene beauty around you, this is the perfect path for you. With beautiful majestic blue water with vibrant green trees, with a nice gentle flow, it is also a great opportunity to chat. It is also the longest trip we have available.
